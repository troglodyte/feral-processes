# Drop pods

A Drop Pod Terminal fires one of your base programs into a fight that is
already going. Research Drop Pods (it needs Dispatch, and zone 2) to build
the terminal, then learn the Call Reinforcements routine. It works on
[battle maps](battle-maps) only; the intrusion screen never offers it.

Who comes is up to you. The base staff table grows a POD column once a
terminal stands: tick it for every program you would trust in a fight. A
ticked program goes on working the base as usual. Ticking it only says it
may be called.

When you run Call Reinforcements, the trooper nearest a charged terminal
drops. On a tie, the one higher on the base staff table goes. It lands
beside you and loses its first turn getting its bearings. After that it
is yours to command like anyone else in your party, even if your party is
already full.

When the fight ends it goes home and back to work, keeping the experience
it earned. A trooper that falls is treated like any other program you
lose.

The call is refused, costing nothing, when:

- no terminal is charged,
- no trooper is at the base and on shift,
- you are defending the base itself,
- or the board has no free cell to land on.

Firing spends the terminal. Your crew reloads it by carrying its bill over,
the same way they supply a build, and its sheet in the structures list
shows how far along the recharge is. A recharge cannot be called off.

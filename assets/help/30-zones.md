# Zones and breaching

A zone is a tier of the Phase-Manifold Basin (the Basin), not a separate place: there is one map for the whole run, and each
breach raises the tier everything on it is scaled against. Every wild program carries the zone it
was born in appended to its name, and each zone up is a step harder than the last — a steady climb,
not a doubling, so the levels it takes to keep pace stay roughly the same however deep you go.

You leave a zone through a Zone Portal. Deploy one and walk onto it.

- A portal costs 24 Portal Fragments and 4 Routine Disks in zone 1. Every line of the price grows by
  half its starting amount for each zone you climb, so the fragments run 24, 36, 48 and on. From
  zone 2 it also asks for 2 Trace Sniffers and 10 Cache Grain, and from zone 3 for 3 Recompile
  Kernels.
- Fragments come out of the Stack and nowhere else. Beating a lair's guardian underground is what
  pays for the way out, which is why a run that never goes down never breaches.
- The portal is consumed as you step through it. It is the one structure that does not make the
  trip, so every breach is a fresh build.
- There is no way back.

A breach destroys nothing you carry: Portal Fragments, Core Fragments, credits, gear and supplies
all stay in your pack. Nothing is teleported either. Your party and every deployed structure stay
exactly where they are, damage, node stock and running cronjobs included. What changes is the
ground around you: wild programs nearby are cleared, and the map re-stocks at the new tier as you
walk it, and Stack entrances are re-built at the new tier.

How much meets you at once is the zone's business, not the map's. Zone 1 sends one group at a
time, never more than two programs, which is what a run needs while your party is still empty;
every zone after that widens the ceiling on how large a group can get and how many groups can join
a fight. A bigger party of your own draws bigger and more numerous groups too.

Distance from home matters a little too. Within seven tiles of a fresh zone-1 landing nothing
spawns that a bare level-1 player could not beat one-on-one. Past that, spawns ramp up in strength
over the next 32 tiles until they sit at the level of the next zone up.

# Attributes

Every program in the Phase-Manifold Basin — and you — carries a second, non-combat stat block. Six numbers that
say what something is *like*, as opposed to what it can do in a fight. Open a program's manifest
and press D to read its dossier.

Each one is named twice. The first name is the Basin's own word for it; the second, in parentheses,
is the old-school word it stands in for. You do not need to know the setting's vocabulary to read
the sheet.

- Analysis (Intelligence) — how well it reads what it is given.
- Persistence (Willpower) — how cleanly it keeps its own state when things go wrong around it.
- Entropy (Luck) — how much accumulated noise it runs with.
- Bandwidth (Stamina) — how much it pushes through at one time.
- Footprint (Size) — how much of the heap it occupies while it runs.
- Parity (Vitality) — how well it catches and corrects its own errors.

Your numbers are where your stat points go: twenty at creation and six at each level-up. Each point
above an attribute's baseline moves the stats it feeds — the Points screen lists them beside each
attribute. Close the level-up page without spending and L on your own manifest brings it back for as
long as points are left to place.

Entropy is the odd one out. Its baseline is 40, and each point above that adds 0.2% to your crit
chance and 0.1% to your fumble chance, so it widens both ends of an attack at once. Crit stops at
20% and fumble at 11%, and both are reached at Entropy 100.

A program's numbers work the same way for it. Each time a program levels up, its points go into
Parity and Analysis, which raise its Integrity and Attack, and Analysis also drives how well a
program posted to a job mines.

A program's numbers start out fixed by where it was and what it is. Two programs of the same species
found in different places are different programs; the same program is the same program after you
save, reload, tame it, post it to a job or field it in your party. They change only when it levels
up. A fused program is a new one and is settled afresh — it inherits neither parent's sheet.

Your own six start from the class you picked at creation. A program's come from its species.

Above the six, the dossier carries a build revision, a checksum and one line of where this thing
came from. None of it is stored anywhere; it is read off the sheet itself.

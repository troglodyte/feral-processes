# Attributes

Every program in the Grid — and you — carries a second, non-combat stat block. Five numbers that
say what something is *like*, as opposed to what it can do in a fight. Open a program's manifest
and press D to read its dossier.

Each one is named twice. The first name is the Grid's own word for it; the second, in parentheses,
is the old-school word it stands in for. You do not need to know the setting's vocabulary to read
the sheet.

- Persistence (Willpower) — how cleanly it keeps its own state when things go wrong around it.
- Entropy (Luck) — how much accumulated noise it runs with.
- Bandwidth (Stamina) — how much it pushes through at one time.
- Footprint (Size) — how much of the heap it occupies while it runs.
- Parity (Vitality) — how well it catches and corrects its own errors.

None of these five does anything yet. No roll reads them, no formula includes them, and no
screen outside the dossier is affected by one. They are there to be read. When a mechanic does
arrive for one, it will be designed around the number that is already on the sheet rather than the
other way round, and this page will say what it does.

A program's numbers are settled the moment it comes into existence, from where it was and what it
is, and they never move again. Two programs of the same species found in different places are
different programs; the same program is the same program after you save, reload, tame it, post it
to a job or field it in your party. A fused program is a new one and is settled afresh — it
inherits neither parent's sheet.

Your own five come from the class you picked at creation. A program's come from its species.

Above the five, the dossier carries a build revision, a checksum and one line of where this thing
came from. None of it is stored anywhere; it is read off the sheet itself.

# The base AI

Somewhere in your first dozen research projects, something at the base wakes up. You will not choose
when. After a handful of projects are done, the program with the sharpest Analysis among your base
staff stops what it is doing, walks to the nearest Research Station and goes quiet. A notification
tells you something is wrong with it. For about a minute of game time nothing is researched anywhere.

Then it fuses with the station. From then on:

- It does all your research, and only at that one Station. It cannot be fielded, posted elsewhere,
  released or sold, and the Station it sits in cannot be taken down. Every other Research Station
  stops producing, and a new one is refused.
- It works at a quarter of the pace it managed before. Research gets slow.
- It gets better by doing the work. Its Analysis climbs as it researches, and its pace climbs with
  it. You can read the figure on its dossier like any other program's.

Its learning stops at a threshold, and the base's alert board will say it is waiting to be trained.
Until you train it, it will not improve, and a few projects stay shut to you.

- Pick "Train your AI" at the end of the research list. It needs a program pinned at the Research
  Station, which is used up when the training is done. Choose that subject with care.
- Each training takes the AI up one level and lets it learn further, until the next threshold.
- Each level opens projects that cannot be started at a lower one. Such a project reads "Seems
  feasible, but we don't know where to start" until the AI reaches its level.

An untrained AI can still finish every project that asks nothing of its level, only slowly. An AI
trained at each threshold ends up faster than your researchers were before it woke.

See [attributes](attributes) for what Analysis does, and [your base](your-base) for the Research
Station.

# Forging weapons

Once you have researched Weapon Fabrication, a Workbench lets you design your own weapons and build
them. Stand beside the Workbench and press c; the weapon pages are Forge, New design and
Salvage. Like every Workbench page they use what the base is storing as well as what you carry.

A design is a saved recipe: one Frame and a few modifiers. The Frame is the weapon's family and its
base numbers: Edge, Lance, Whip or Launcher, each in versions. Modifiers change how it behaves: a
longer range, a wider reach, a damage type, a rider that has a chance to set a status on a hit, an
exploit that hits harder against a target already carrying a status, a tilt toward accuracy or
toward punch, a decompiler, or a routine the weapon runs. You can also add affixes you know.
A design holds only so many modifiers; research raises the limit.

- New design page. Press Enter on a row to see the parts that fit it. Type to filter the list. A part
  that does not fit is greyed and says why. Name the design, then choose Save. Saving costs nothing.
  The preview underneath reads out what the weapon does, its rating, which parts a build uses and
  which you are short of. Under Materials it lists what the build would spend, in green when you have
  enough and red when you are short. Each part in the list shows what it costs to craft.
- Forge page. Your saved designs. Press Enter to build one weapon. It uses any parts you hold and crafts the ones
  you are missing from materials. It also uses the Frame's
  material, and the weapon lands in your pack. The highlighted design shows its Materials underneath.
  A part you have not researched cannot be crafted; research it first. A proc part comes only
  from the Salvage page. Press D to delete a design. A design cannot be deleted while a weapon built from it is in your pack, worn, on the floor or on a
  trader's shelf.
- Salvage page. Takes a weapon you found apart. The weapon is lost, but you get the parts it was made
  of, learn how to craft them, and learn any affixes it carried. Weapons built at the Workbench
  cannot be taken apart.

A weapon you build is ordinary gear with one exception: it can never be fused. Only weapons you found
can be fused.

The Weapon research screen, opened from the research page, holds the research for new parts and for
a bigger modifier limit. Study programs to discover more of it.

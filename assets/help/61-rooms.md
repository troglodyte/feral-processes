# Rooms and doors

A room is a stretch of base floor with walls or rock all the way around it. Nobody builds one
directly: close off a space and the base notices it. A gap in the wall, or a corridor that runs on
into open floor, means the space is not a room yet.

A door is how the crew gets through. Build one in a gap in the wall and the programs walk through
it, while the space behind it still counts as closed. Every other machine and bench blocks the
crew, so a door is the only thing that can stand in a doorway. During a siege a door is an
obstacle like any other structure.

What a room is for depends on what stands in it.

- quarters: exactly one Log Analyzer Bay.
- dormitory: two or more Log Analyzer Bays.
- rec room: any recreation machine.
- workshop: any bench that makes things.

A room that matches more than one reads as the more important use. A room with nothing in it, or
the open floor outside, has no role and no effect. The same goes for the space around your Home:
whatever you build in that first pocket never makes a room, unless you wall it off from the
Home's space.

A room with a role also has a quality: Cramped, Plain, Fine or Superb. It comes from how much
floor the room has, how much of that floor is laid finish rather than bare tile, and how many
machines are crowded into it.

- Cramped rooms refill needs more slowly, and programs standing in one feel it.
- Plain rooms change nothing.
- Fine and Superb rooms refill needs faster, and programs standing in one feel better for it.
- A workshop works faster the better it is. The speed is fixed when a job is posted, so a change
  shows from the next job on.
- Two programs sharing a dormitory or quarters notice each other: friends are glad of it, rivals
  are not.

Hold Alt to see it. Every room is tinted by its role and labelled with its quality and role, such
as Fine dormitory. Inspecting a machine also says which room it is in.

What a refilled need and a good mood do for the crew is in [staff morale](staff-morale).

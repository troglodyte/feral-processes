# Implants

An implant is a piece of hardware built into your own body. It changes what you can do for as long
as it stays in, and it asks something of you in return. Research Wetware Splicing and build a
Splice Rig in your [base](your-base). Implants themselves are forged at a Fabricator from that
research.

Stand beside a Splice Rig and press I. The screen lists what is already built in, then the
implants in your pack. Pick a row, then press I to install it or R to take one out. The screen
shows what each one does to you, what it drinks, and its downside if it has one.

## Load

Everything built in adds to your Neural Load, shown as used over cap. The cap starts low and rises
as you level, so a stronger body can carry more. Implants also drain your Power faster the more
Load you carry, and Low Power Mode eases that drain along with the rest. Each row shows its own
share as a percent.

You may install past the cap. The game asks first, because overloading is a gamble: any fight you
start over the cap can open with a rejection, a nasty status that lands on you before you have
done anything. The further over you are, the likelier it is, up to a ceiling. While you are over,
a LOAD tag shows on the top bar, and the points screen shows the same Load line.

## Downsides

Some implants carry a downside of their own: Trace that climbs faster, or a chance to open a fight
with a status on you. These count whether or not you are overloaded. A few implants do the
opposite and slow Trace down.

## Taking one out

Removal is priced in core fragments, a few for every point of Load the implant carries, paid from
your pack. The implant comes back to your pack as an item and nothing else is refunded. Core
fragments are local to the zone you are in and are lost at a [breach](extraction), so keep some
back if you mean to re-fit before then. An implant the game no longer recognises shows as unknown:
it does nothing, and it comes out for free.

You cannot splice or remove anything in the middle of a fight.

# Sieges

Your base is something other things want. Wild programs lay siege to it, a program that beat you
can come back with a band to do the same, and a settlement you have wronged can send raiders for
your build currency. Wild sieges and the Entropy Sweep wait for the second zone; a nemesis or a
wronged settlement can come in any zone. A siege or a nemesis march also holds off until your base
is established, at 8 staff and 8 structures.

The wild siege builds on its own clock. In zone 2 one comes roughly once an hour of play, and sooner
the deeper you are. The log and the alert board warn you that movement is gathering at the perimeter
before it comes: about twelve minutes out in zone 2, and never less than eight however deep you are.
That is the time to walk home.

A siege is a pack of wild programs, four plus one for every zone, up to twelve. Which fight you get
depends on where you are.

- Home: the fight is a battle map drawn from your base. The rooms and corridors you dug are the
  board, your machines are obstacles the raiders can smash, and your staff defend where they stand
  under their own control. A raider killed while carrying loot drops it again, so the fight is about
  catching them before they reach the door. It is always a battle map, even with battle maps turned
  off. The raiders withdraw once half of them are down or gone out the door with their plunder. See
  [battle maps](battle-maps).
- Away: it is settled without you, set against your staff, your defences and your Turrets. Whatever
  the defence fails to cover is taken from your stores, broken off your machines, and can bench
  staff. A siege you are not home for can still damage your walls.

A Turret fires on the nearest besieger in range every round of a siege, and it also soaks damage
from a sweep. Walls and doors stand in the raiders' way, and a wall stops blocking sight once it
breaks. A siege in progress is kept in your save.

The nemesis is the other source. When you jack out of a fight, or lose one, the programs still
standing become nemeses: each gains a grudge, rises one rarity, heals fully, and the first time it
is also given a name. A nemesis then gathers followers, one about every five minutes, up to three,
taking wild programs near it or spawning one beside it. When the band is full the alert board tells
you it has gathered, and after another eight minutes it marches on your base as a siege, the leader
and three followers.

If you are at home, you fight that band on the same battle map. If you are away, it is settled
off-screen at the band's size. If the leader survives, whether the siege was won or the band lost
heart, it walks back to where it started, one grudge worse and a rarity higher, and starts gathering
again. If you decompile the leader in the fight, it is yours and the march ends.

A GC Entropy Sweep is a different event. It damages structures and never comes inside. One builds
slowly toward every fifteen to twenty-five minutes in zone 2, sooner the deeper you are. The base
tells you about four minutes ahead: telemetry thickens around the anchor, the log says so and an
alert stays up until it hits. A sweep only lands when at least five of your staff are on their feet
and not down, so benching your crew does not make you safe. The sweep waits for the first moment the
base can answer for itself, and it keeps the pressure it built.

Raiders from a settlement you have wronged come half as often as a sweep. They take a share of
your banked build currency rather than breaking anything, and every point of raid defence you have,
from a Shield or a Turret, cuts that share. Raids on an outpost work differently; see [outposts](outposts).

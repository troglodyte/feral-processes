# Outposts

An outpost is a second foothold out on the surface — a fixture you found on open ground, well
clear of your base, that a crew of your own programs can work while you are somewhere else
entirely.

Research Outpost Founding and you can compile an Outpost Kit. Carry it out to a stretch of open,
walkable ground away from your base, a settlement, a nest or the Stack, and use it there. It
refuses on a Platform biome, too close to your anchor, or too close to another outpost, and it is
never spent unless the founding actually takes.

Walking onto a founded outpost opens its own page.

- P posts a base-staff program there. It stops counting toward your labour pool at home the moment
  it is posted — the same trade a sortie or a wield makes.
- U recalls the highlighted crew member back to ordinary staff.
- c opens the same take-only transfer screen your Depots use, straight onto the outpost's stock.
- R repairs a damaged outpost from your own pack, once it has taken a hit.

Growth runs in three tiers — raw, processed, complex — and each tier's crew produces everything
that tier and every tier below it can yield, one pick per cycle. Growth needs crew to keep moving;
short a body and it holds where it is rather than falling back a tier outright, and the top tier
needs the most hands. The page tells you which of four things is happening to it right now, and
why: growing, holding steady at what the crew posted can reach, sitting on a full stock with
nowhere to put more, or slipping — undercrewed, damaged, or backed up too long.

Getting the stock home is your choice. Walk it out by hand through that same take screen, or send a
caravan: at a Relay, C on a known outpost dispatches one the way it does to a settlement, except
there is no manifest to build — it goes out empty and hauls back a load on its own. A standing
route keeps making that same round trip on its own until you cut it.

An outpost can be raided exactly like your base once your run has reached zone 2 — the first zone
never rolls one at all. A hit costs it some integrity, steals a share of its stock, and benches one
crew member; enough hits without a repair and it goes dark, sending every crew member still posted
there home and halting it until R brings it back.

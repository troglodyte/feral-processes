# The Stack

The Stack is underneath the sector, and it is where Portal Fragments come from. Nothing on the
surface pays them, so a run that never goes down never breaches. It is also the only place in the
game where Power is genuinely scarce, and that is the whole character of the place.

Three links open onto it per zone, drawn as a magenta > on the map, and they are deliberately few
enough that finding one is a thing you do rather than a thing that happens. The farther a link
stands from where your run began, the deeper the stack under it runs — two frames at the least, six
at the most.

Inside, you are looking down a corridor rather than at a map. hjkl and the arrow keys move you
forward and back and turn you left and right, g draws what you have walked so far, and > and < take
you down a frame and up one. Climbing from the top frame is how you leave.

What a frame holds:

- Caches, sitting in dead ends. Walking onto one empties it, and they are the biggest single source
  of noise down here.
- Doors, which you cannot see past — that is the point of them. A sealed door has to be shouldered
  open, and costs nothing but the noise of doing it.
- Faults, holes that drop you to the frame below, landing far from that frame's way up. Terrain,
  not a one-shot: a fault works every time.
- Corruption, rotten substrate that costs Integrity while you stand on it. A stretch of it is a
  route you can decide to walk around.
- Breakpoints, which map the whole frame at a stroke and tell the Stack exactly where you are —
  the single loudest thing you can do down there, and one use each.
- Orphans — a process left running with nothing left to serve. o adopts one for a catalyst rather
  than for a won fight. What species it is was decided by the place, so it is the same one every
  time you come back.
- Traders, on junctions rather than down spurs. They keep no buyback, the shelf does not refill, and
  not every frame has one.
- The lair, on the bottom frame, where the way down would otherwise be. Walking in starts the
  guardian fight.

Trace is how loud you have been, and it has four bands. Everything you take or force raises it — a
breakpoint loudest of all, then cracking a cache or jumping blind, then forcing a seal; a kill
barely registers. As it climbs, encounters come more often, packs come larger, and every hostile
down there including the guardian hits harder. It resets the moment you surface, so a Stack run is
a decision about when to leave with what you have.

Beating a lair's guardian is the point of the descent. It pays Portal Fragments and it is the only
thing in the game that drops a Privilege Ring. It also brings the stack down behind it: the link you
came in by collapses, and a fresh one opens elsewhere in the sector with an uncleared lair under it.
That is what makes a zone's fragment supply renewable rather than fixed, and it is why spending
fragments at a bench can never strand a run.

Two warnings. A guardian cannot be decompiled, whatever you spend on it. And a Recharger Node cannot
reach you down here, so the Power you carry in is the Power you have.

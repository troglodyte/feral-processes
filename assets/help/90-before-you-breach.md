# Before you breach

There is no way back through a portal, so the question of whether you are ready for the next zone is
worth asking before you build one rather than after you step through. What changes on the far side
is not just that everything hits harder. It is the shape of the fights.

Before zone 2, the thing to have is a routine that hits a whole group.

Zone 1 fields one program at a time, whatever else is going on, which is why a run's opening reads
as a duel. Zone 2 lifts that to ten in a group and two groups on the field at once. A party that
only knows how to hit one target answers a group of ten one program at a time while all ten answer
back, and that arithmetic does not improve by playing it better. A routine whose name ends in Group
is the answer, and it is worth going into the Stack for the Portal Fragments knowing you also need
the levels, the disks or the research to bring one across.

Take across as well:

- A party. Five can fight beside you, and past zone 1 you want the front slot filled by something
  that can hold it while the rest work.
- Power to spend. Group routines cost far more than a swing, and Power is what pays for them.
- Gear worth re-equipping. A piece locks in the level and zone you equipped it at, so re-equipping
  everything the moment you land is free strength.

Before zone 3, the thing to have is a routine that hits everything.

Zone 3 fields three groups and zone 4 fields four, which is the ceiling. Only the front two are ever
in reach of you at once — the rest can act only with routines that reach that far, and they come
forward as you clear the ones in front. A routine whose name ends in Everyone stops being a luxury
here, because a fight where three groups take turns being the front one is a fight decided by how
much of the field you can touch per turn.

The research that teaches the widest of them sits behind zone 3 itself and a good deal of Research
Data, so a Research Node running through zone 2 is the thing that makes zone 3 survivable rather
than something you set up after arriving.

Two more things worth doing before the step, both of them about what does not survive it:

- Spend your Core Fragments and Portal Fragments. Neither crosses. Compile the catalysts, the cells
  and the upgrades you were saving them for, and sell whatever is left.
- Check your companions against the ceiling. A companion stops at level 6, and the only thing that
  lifts it is a Privilege Ring off a lair guardian. If a program is capped and you are still
  climbing, it is falling behind every zone you cross — refactor it, ring it, fuse it, or plan on
  replacing it.

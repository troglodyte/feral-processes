Roster Stat Sheet

Every shipped species in feral-processes, plotted from its own .ron. Where a file omits a field the engine default is shown: base_speed 10, growth_multiplier 1.0, all five affinities 1.0. POW is the engine's own scalar, Stats::power = max_hp + atk + def.

Attack against defense

Bubble area tracks base HP. The two bosses carry a ring. The cluster at ATK 10–11 is the mid roster — what separates those five is entirely HP and DEF.

Stat profiles

One row order throughout — descending POW — so a row's shape across the four bars is the species' character. Each bar is scaled against the roster maximum for that stat, not against the others.

Taming cost against growth tier

taming_difficulty along the axis, growth_multiplier as the lane. The two move together across the whole roster: nothing is cheap to compile and steep to level, and nothing is expensive and flat.

Where they live, what they yield

Four walkable biomes host spawns. Both bosses list all four; seven species are single-biome. A yield is what the species produces when posted to a work node.

Full sheet

Every field, as parsed. NEST marks can_nest; affinities are listed only where the file overrides the 1.0 baseline.

Source of truth is assets/species/ — a mod dropping a file in that directory joins this roster without a recompile. Speed is an initiative baseline, not a turn order: every combatant rolls base_speed + d10 each round, and the player rolls from 11.
